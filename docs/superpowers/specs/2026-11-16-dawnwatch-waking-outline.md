# Outline: The Dawnwatch, Book One: Waking

Date: 2026-11-16.
Status: draft. The human reviews it before the chapters are written.

Parent design: [The Dawnwatch](2026-11-16-the-dawnwatch-design.md).

This file gives the plan of each chapter of Book One. The chapters use
milestone levels: the characters go up one level at the end of each
chapter.

## Threads through the book

### The black stone

The party finds one black stone in the tutorial. It is a piece of the
crystal of Malgrath. Chapter 4 gives it rules: the bearer gets a Mark that
grows. The Hush wants the stone. Chapter 10 tells what it is.

### The pieces of the Dawnfire

Ostrey split the Dawnfire into pieces and kept each piece in a different
place. The piece in the chapel of the keep went out in the tutorial. Book
One finds three pieces:

1. **The Ember of Saint Ivo.** The Church keeps it as a holy relic.
   Chapter 3.
2. **The First Lamp.** The guild of lantern makers keeps it in Corrantey.
   Chapter 6.
3. **The piece in Fennick.** It is under the water. Chapter 8.

Wick is the last true piece. Nobody knows this in Book One.

### The steward

The steward of Dawnwatch Keep made Wick and put the Dawnwatch to sleep.
The tutorial does not give the steward a name. Book One calls the steward
**Haskel Orme**. Letters from the steward are handouts in chapters 7 and
10. They show the lie, step by step.

### The Before and After threads

Each pregenerated character has a "Before" question. Each chapter has a box
for one thread. The GM uses the answers of the players.

| Character | Before question (short) | Chapter of the thread |
|---|---|---|
| The Shield | A promise to someone | 9 |
| The Light | A sign from a god | 3 |
| The Spark | A spell on the first day | 5 |
| The Shadow | One last thing stolen | 2 |
| The Storm | Why the character agreed to sleep | 8 |

For a character that the players make themselves, the GM uses the box with
the nearest question.

## The chapters, one by one

### Chapter 1: The Last Watch (levels 1 to 2)

The tutorial, with no change to its lessons. At the end, one new paragraph
leads into chapter 2.

### Chapter 2: Brannock's Ford (levels 2 to 3)

- **Goal:** show the 300 years, and teach travel.
- The first village. The coins of the party are 300 years old. The people
  speak with a different accent. A song about the Dawnwatch has all the
  facts wrong.
- A pedlar sells "bones of the Five Sleepers" to pilgrims.
- **Journeys starts:** three days on the road to Saint Ivo's, with roles,
  weather, and a supply die.
- **Thread (the Shadow):** the thing that the character stole is now an
  old treasure. A collector offers a lot of money for it.
- **People:** Tamsin, the old ferry woman; Dace, the pedlar of bones.

### Chapter 3: Saint Ivo's (levels 3 to 4)

- **Goal:** the party meets the Church, and the people who want to use
  them.
- The town has its big feast for the Five Sleepers. A troupe of actors says
  that they are the Dawnwatch.
- The party must prove who they are. The Ember of Saint Ivo glows near
  them.
- **Thread (the Light):** the sign of the god is in a window of the
  church, made 200 years after the sleep.
- **People:** Abbess Merrow, who wants the party as living saints; Brother
  Cade, who thinks that they are impostors; Garret Pell, the leader of the
  troupe.

### Chapter 4: The Hush (levels 4 to 5)

- **Goal:** the end of Act I. The cult of the Unlit King comes out.
- The Hush steals the Ember of Saint Ivo and tries to take the black stone.
- The party follows them to the old shrine at Kell.
- **Marks starts:** the black stone gives its bearer a Mark. It grows when
  the bearer uses the stone.
- At the end, the party learns that Malgrath can feel where the stone is.
- **People:** Mother Sallow, the leader of the Hush. She escapes.

### Chapter 5: Gaunt (levels 5 to 6)

- **Goal:** the start of Act II. Politics and power.
- Marshal Ide Gauntry wants the Dawnwatch as banners for her army.
- **Factions and renown starts:** the party gets ranks with Gaunt, with the
  Church, or with both.
- **Thread (the Spark):** the war mages of Gaunt teach a "Dawnwatch spell".
  It is the spell from the first day of the Spark, and they cast it wrong.
- **People:** Marshal Ide Gauntry; her son Tobin, who does not want a war.

### Chapter 6: Corrantey (levels 6 to 7)

- **Goal:** the second piece of the Dawnfire, and the past of Wick.
- The guild of lantern makers sees the mark of its maker on Wick: the mark
  of the steward.
- The First Lamp is in the hall of the guild. Agents of Gaunt want it too.
- **Crafting and gathering starts:** the guild makes items from the
  materials that the party brings.
- **People:** Perrin Hale, the master of the guild.

### Chapter 7: The Keep Again (levels 7 to 8)

- **Goal:** a home.
- The Hush holds Dawnwatch Keep. They search the Hall of Sleepers.
- The party takes back the keep.
- **Strongholds starts:** the keep becomes the base of the party.
- The party finds the closed study of the steward. The first letters are
  handouts.

### Chapter 8: Fennick Under the Water (levels 8 to 9)

- **Goal:** the third piece of the Dawnfire. A dungeon.
- Fennick was a town of Ostrey. A flood covered it 200 years ago.
- **Techniques starts:** an old soldier of Ostrey, a ghost, teaches the
  party the old fighting ways.
- **Thread (the Storm):** the reason why the character agreed to sleep is
  in Fennick.

### Chapter 9: Three Banners (levels 9 to 10)

- **Goal:** the party chooses a side, or stops a war.
- Gaunt and Saint Ivo's go to war. Each says that the Dawnwatch is on its
  side. The Hush helps both sides to fight.
- **Injuries and scars starts:** this is the first true battle.
- **Thread (the Shield):** the promise of the character comes back. A
  family that came from the person of the promise is in the war.

### Chapter 10: The Empty Crystal (level 10)

- **Goal:** the end of Book One.
- The party attacks the Hush in Kingsholm, the old capital of Ostrey.
- **The victory:** the party breaks the Hush and defeats Mother Sallow.
- **The twist:** under Kingsholm is the vault where Malgrath slept. The
  crystal is empty. On it is the badge of the Dawnwatch. The last letter of
  the steward tells the truth: Malgrath was the first Dawnwatch.
- The last words of the book are from Malgrath: "Now you know what we
  are."

## New content for Book One

The book uses the monsters and the NPCs of the two Compendiums where it
can. It adds only what the story needs:

- **Creatures:** the Hush (cultists at three tiers, and Mother Sallow), the
  ghosts of Fennick, and one creature for each act end.
- **People:** each named NPC with a role in a fight gets a statblock.
- **Treasures:** the black stone (rules from chapter 4), the three pieces
  of the Dawnfire, and one unique item for each chapter.
