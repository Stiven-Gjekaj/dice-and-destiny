## Originality

The SRD has no Battle Master. I did not copy or rename a paid maneuver. I
removed my drafts that were too near to paid maneuvers or feats (feint with
an extra die, disarm, parry, riposte, rally, sweep, trip, a reaction attack
that sets Speed to 0, a bonus attack with the haft). Near SRD content:
Refuse to Fall is like Relentless Endurance (the Orc species), at 3 points
and level 11.

## Budget (1 point = about one extra weapon die)

Strongest entries, with the SRD entry that each is equal to:

- Mountain Stance (2 points, level 11): Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing,
  and Slashing for 1 minute; Speed halved; no Reactions. Compare: Rage
  (Barbarian), with real costs. The strongest stance. Tell me if it must be
  3 points or level 17.
- Storm Stance (2, level 11): one more attack on a Critical Hit or a kill,
  once each turn. Compare: the Cleave weapon mastery, once each turn.
- Turtle Stance (1): +2 AC for 1 minute, but only one attack each turn.
  Compare: Shield spell (but only with the cost of the lost attacks).
- Tiger Stance (2, level 5): +1d6 once each turn, -2 AC.
- Finish Them (2, level 5): +2d8 against a Bloodied creature. Compare:
  Divine Smite with a level 1 slot (2d8).
- Thunderous Charge (2, level 5): +2d6 and Prone (Strength save) after a
  20-foot charge.
- Covering Fire (1): a Reaction ranged attack when a creature moves toward
  an ally. Compare: an Opportunity Attack at range.
- Perfect Strike (3, level 17): if the attack hits, it is a Critical Hit.
- Blinding Dust (1): Blinded until the start of your next turn (Dexterity
  save). Compare: the level 1 spell Color Spray in an older form; here one
  creature, a short time.
