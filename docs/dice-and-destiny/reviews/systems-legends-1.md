## Budget (Tier I to V = Common, Uncommon, Rare, Very Rare, Legendary)

Most tiers give an SRD spell once each Long Rest, at the level that the
budget allows: Tier II a level 1 to 2 spell, Tier III a level 3 to 4 spell,
Tier IV a level 5 to 6 spell, Tier V a level 7 or more spell (some once each
7 days). All 42 spells that the paths name are in the SRD data. Spells of
level 8 and 9 are once each 7 days (Dominate Monster, Antimagic Field,
Control Weather, Foresight). Resurrection (level 7) is once each 7 days.

Strongest entries, with the SRD entry that each is equal to:

- Dragonslayer IV: Critical Hit on 19 or 20 against Dragons, Immunity to
  Frightened. Compare: the Champion's Improved Critical, only against one
  creature type. V: once each Long Rest, no damage from one breath weapon,
  allies within 10 feet take half.
- Deathless IV: the first death after this tier, return at the next dawn
  with 1 HP (once). V: stop aging; once each Long Rest, drop to 1 HP when
  killed outright. Compare: Legendary items such as the Ring of Three Wishes
  are stronger; this is a strong defensive Legendary.
- Liberator IV: allies within 30 feet can't be Charmed or Frightened while
  you are conscious. Compare: the Paladin's Aura of Courage, wider.
  V: once each Long Rest, end six conditions on all chosen allies within 60
  feet, and give Temporary HP equal to your level.
- Spellbreaker IV: Advantage on saves against spells (compare Mantle of
  Spell Resistance, Rare) and Counterspell once each Long Rest.
- Peacemaker III: once each Long Rest, chosen creatures within 30 feet
  can't attack for 1 minute (Wisdom save; ends on damage). Compare:
  Hypnotic Pattern (level 3). V: the same, 300 feet and 1 hour, once each
  7 days.
- Shadow III: always under Nondetection (level 3).
