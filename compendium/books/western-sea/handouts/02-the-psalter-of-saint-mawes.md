# The Psalter of Saint Mawes

*The last page of a small book of psalms bound in sealskin, from the tomb
under the altar of the church on Founders' Hill. After the dawn hymn, a few
lines in a small, careful hand. The ink has gone the color of rust.*

---

> **The Dawn Hymn**
>
> *Morning Flame, that wakes the world,*
> *come softly to the sleeping.*
> *We hold no light that is not thine.*
> *We give the dark thy keeping.*
>
> *Lie still, lie still, the night is long,*
> *the sea is deep and kind.*
> *Lie still, lie still, till morning come,*
> *and leave the dark behind.*

---

> It is not land. It sleeps.
>
> I made no light upon it after, and I went down to it in the dark, and I
> was still, and it was still.
>
> *M.*
