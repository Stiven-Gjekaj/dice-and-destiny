# The Log of the Watch

*A page from the oldest books of the log of the watch, in the turf hut at
the foot of the Corrin Light. Most pages say "Wind west. Nothing." This
one is from the year 211 of the Landing. The hand shakes.*

---

> **The Turning. The fourth night.**
>
> Wind all round the compass. Rain. The Wall on the island.
>
> Lit at the turn of the night with all the oil of Hythe. At the second
> hour the Wall opened to the east, a lane of quiet water, and stars over
> it. The *Hopewell* went into it under all sail.
>
> The ground rose under the tower as the lane opened, a long slow lifting,
> and I held to the wall.
>
> I write this so that it is written. God bring them home.
>
> *W. Hocking, of the watch*
