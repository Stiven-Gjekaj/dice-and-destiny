# Pell's Players

*A playbill, nailed to every door in Saint Ivo's for the Feast of the Sleepers.*

> **PELL'S PLAYERS**
> *By Appointment to the Pilgrim Road*
>
> **PRESENT, FOR THE FEAST OF THE SLEEPERS**
>
> # THE FIVE SLEEPERS
> ## As They Truly Were, and As They Truly Are
>
> See the Battle of Ashby Field!
> See the Hundred Hounds of the Unlit King!
> See the Gate Held Until the Dawn!
>
> **AND, FOR THIS FEAST ONLY:**
> As the Holy Ember blazes, the Five have woken beneath the holy hill,
> and they have come home to their Church!
> Look upon the faces of the Sleepers, and be blessed!
>
> **Garret Pell** as the Shield, the First of the Five
> **Tilly Ashe** as the Shadow, who stole the King's Crown
> **Mattock Brand** as the Storm
> **Rosalind Vey** as the Light
> **Pip Corder** as the Spark *(with real Fire!)*
> and **Old Benno** as the Unlit King *(on Stilts)*
>
> In the Players' Yard of the Lamb and Lantern,
> each night of the feast, at the evening bell.
> **Two coppers. Children free. No cabbages before the second act.**
>
> *Wake well, and see the morning!*
