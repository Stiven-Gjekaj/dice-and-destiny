# Two Proclamations

*Two sheets of paper, one from each group of riders. Copies of both hang
side by side on the doors of the inns along the road to Ashby Field.*

---

## By the Order of the Marshal of Gaunt

**To all the people of the south, the free towns, and the lands of old
Ostrey:**

Know that the Five Sleepers of the Dawnwatch are awake. They walk the land
again, as the old books said they would.

Know also that the Dawnwatch were soldiers. They swore their oath to the
crown of Ostrey, and Gaunt holds the last true banner of that crown. A
soldier serves the crown. The Dawnwatch serve with us.

The priests of Saint Ivo's say that the Sleepers belong to their altar. They
have burned the chapel at Wenlow and called it the work of Gaunt. They lie,
as they have lied for a hundred and sixty years.

Therefore the army of Gaunt marches north to Ashby Field, to keep the
border, to protect the pilgrims, and to stand beside the Dawnwatch as
Ostrey stood beside them.

Each loyal person who brings food, horses, or iron to the army will be paid
in good Gaunt silver.

*Given at Stannard, under my hand and seal,*

**Ide Gauntry, Marshal of Gaunt, Warden of the South**

---

## A Letter of the Church to the Faithful

**To the children of the Five Sleepers, in every town and every field:**

Rejoice. The Sleepers have woken. The Ember of Saint Ivo glowed when they
came to our door, and the faithful saw it with their own eyes.

The Sleepers are holy. They slept for us, as Saint Ivo watched for us. They
were not made to carry the flags of soldiers, and no Marshal can own them.

Yet Gaunt sends an army north, and an envoy of Gaunt came to our gate with
threats. Gaunt says that the Sleepers march with it. This is not true. The
Sleepers came first to Saint Ivo's, to the shrine that kept their memory
alive.

Therefore the Church calls each faithful person who can hold a spear to
gather at Ashby Field, under the banner of the Five, to keep the holy
road open and the Sleepers free.

Pray for peace. Bring a spear.

*Written in the Church of the Five at Saint Ivo's,*

**Merrow, Abbess of Saint Ivo's, Keeper of the Ember**
