# The Grey Ledger

*The last page of a small book bound in grey cloth, found in a tent at Kell. The hand is small and neat. Some lines are crossed out with a single straight stroke.*

> **The lights that keep him sleeping.**
>
> <del>The chapel of the keep in the Wolds.</del> Done, on the night of the waking.
>
> <del>The ring at Kell.</del> Dead since the long winter. A good bowl for the rite.
>
> The Ember of the saint. At the feast. Bring it to Kell, with the stone.
>
> The lamp of the lantern makers, in their hall by the sea. Well guarded. Patience.
>
> The church under the water, in the fen. Nobody can reach it. Somebody must.
>
> *How many more? He will tell us. He always tells us.*
>
> *The sun is dying. Only the king can save it. Be quiet, and be kind, and put out the light.*
