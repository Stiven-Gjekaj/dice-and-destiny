# The Black Stone

*A Mark card. Keep it with your character sheet. Write your name on it.*

**Bearer:** ________________________________

**Kind:** Taint (open)

> A smooth black stone as big as a plum. It is always cold, and it hums against the skin. Sometimes, when you hold it, you feel it breathe.

**Benefit.** You have Darkvision out to 60 feet, or 30 feet more if you already have it.
Once between Short or Long Rests, as a Bonus Action, you can **draw on the stone** for 1 minute:

- Your weapon attacks and cantrips deal an extra **1d6 Necrotic** damage on a hit.
- Magical Darkness doesn't stop your Darkvision.

**Cost.**

- The grey folk can feel the stone. A member of the Hush within 1 mile of you knows the direction to you.
- You have Disadvantage on saving throws against the Charmed and Frightened conditions from the Hush.
- A piece of the Dawnfire burns you. When you touch one, you take 1d6 Radiant damage.

**Grows.** Each time you draw on the stone. At most once each scene. A partner can spend a Bond to stop it from growing, as with each Taint.

**Track:** [ 0 ] [ 1 ] [ **2** ] [ 3 ] [ **4** ] [ **5** ]

- **At 2:** You dream of cold crystal each night. Once between Long Rests, you can hold the stone and learn the direction to the nearest piece of the Dawnfire within 10 miles. The extra damage becomes **1d8**.
- **At 4:** Your shadow falls a moment late. A member of the Hush thinks that you are one of them until you attack it. You have Disadvantage on attack rolls in the Bright Light of a piece of the Dawnfire.
- **At 5:** The crisis. Your GM tells you what happens. You choose.

**Ending.** Hold the stone in the light of a piece of the Dawnfire from dusk to dawn. You take 1d6 Radiant damage at the start of each hour, and this damage can't be reduced. At dawn, the Mark ends.
