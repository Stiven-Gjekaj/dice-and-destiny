# A Letter from Stannard

*Heavy cream paper, folded twice. The seal is red wax with an iron gauntlet pressed into it.*

> To the Five, called the Sleepers, at the Saints' House in Saint Ivo's.
>
> Light to you.
>
> I use the old greeting on purpose. My house used it for a hundred years before the Church taught the land to say "wake well". I am told that you still say it. I am told a great many things about you, and I would like to know which of them are true.
>
> My family held the March of Gauntry for the kings of Ostrey. When Ostrey fell, we did not. We kept the roads, the law, and the drill. We kept faith with the old kingdom when nobody else did. In my hall at Stannard there is an empty pole, and it has waited two hundred years for the banner of Ostrey.
>
> The Hush stole from the Church under the noses of four hundred guards. The Church has candles. I have soldiers. Come to Stannard, and see what an heir of Ostrey looks like.
>
> The bearer of this letter carries your passes. The road is safe. I keep it so.
>
> And dawn to you.
>
> *Ide Gauntry*
> *Marshal of Gaunt, Warden of the March*
