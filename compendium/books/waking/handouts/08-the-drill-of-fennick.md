# The Drill of Fennick

*Chalked in frost on the wall of the bell tower, in a square, hard hand.*

---

**THIRD WATCH OF FENNICK. MORNING DRILL. NO EXCUSES.**

**1. KNOW YOUR TRICKS.**
A soldier knows as many tricks as years of good service, and one more.
Learn them. Use them. A trick you don't use is a trick you don't know.

**2. COUNT YOUR BREATH.**
You have only so much in you before you must stop and breathe.
When you sit down and catch your wind, it all comes back.
So catch your wind. Every chance you get. That is an order.

**3. ONE AT A TIME.**
One trick on your own turn. One more when the enemy moves.
Not two. Not three. You are a soldier, not a juggler.

**4. STAND RIGHT.**
A stance is how you stand. You take it, and you keep it, until you change it
or until somebody knocks you flat.
One stance at a time. Pick one and mean it.

**5. MOVE RIGHT.**
A move is one thing, at one moment.
When you hit. When they swing. When they charge.
Wait for the moment. Then do it. Then do it again tomorrow.

**6. THE OLD FAVORITES OF THE WATCH.**

- *Stand in front of your friends.* (Guardian's Stance)
- *Point out. They stop running.* (Set Against the Charge)
- *Hit them with the shield, too.* (Shield Rattle)
- *Push them off the wall.* (Driving Strike)
- *Don't block with your face. Turn the blade.* (Turn the Blade)

**7. THE ALL-CLEAR.**
Three ropes. Three hands. One breath.
ONE, TWO, THREE.
The water's gone down. Go home.

---

*Under it, smaller, rubbed out and chalked again many times:*

*Kettle, M., Sgt. Still on watch.*
