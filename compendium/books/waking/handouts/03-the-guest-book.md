# The Guest Book of the Shrine of Saint Ivo

*The last page of a book bound in brown leather, found on a shelf in the old chapel. The ink is faded to the color of tea.*

> **The house of Saint Ivo. Those who rested here.**
>
> Two carters of the Dunn valley, with a load of wool, bound for Kingsholm. One night. They paid in bits.
>
> A woman of the Wolds and her son, who has the cough. Three nights. The water of the saint did him good.
>
> A knight of the north road and his squire. One night. The knight snored. The bees did not like him.
>
