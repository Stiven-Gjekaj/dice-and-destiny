# Player cheat sheet

Print one copy for each player. Everything here is in the 2024 rules.

## The one rule

You tell the DM what your character does. The DM tells you if you must roll.

## Distance

This game uses no feet and no squares. It uses three words:

- **Close:** next to a creature. You need this for a melee attack.
- **Near:** in the same room. One move takes you there.
- **Far:** at the other end of a big hall, or out of the room. A Dash takes
  you there.

You can shoot or cast a spell at any creature that you can see.

## The d20 test

1. Roll a d20 (the big die with 20 sides).
2. Add the number from your sheet.
3. Tell the DM the total.
4. If the total is equal to or more than the target number, you succeed.

There are three types of d20 tests:

| Test | When | Target number |
|------|------|---------------|
| Ability check | You try something hard: climb, sneak, lie, remember. | Difficulty Class (DC) |
| Attack roll | You try to hit an enemy. | The enemy's Armor Class (AC) |
| Saving throw | Something bad happens to you, and you try to resist it. | DC |

**Typical DCs:** 10 is easy. 15 is medium. 20 is hard.

## Advantage and disadvantage

- **Advantage:** roll two d20s and use the higher one.
- **Disadvantage:** roll two d20s and use the lower one.
- If you have both, they cancel. Roll one d20.

## Heroic Inspiration

The DM gives you Heroic Inspiration for good roleplay or a great idea. When
you roll any die, you can use it to roll that die again. You must use the new
roll. You can have only one at a time.

## Your turn in combat

On your turn, you can do each of these one time, in any order:

- **Move:** go to one place near you. For example, go close to an enemy, or
  go behind cover. You can move before or after your action.
- **Action:** one main thing. See the list below.
- **Bonus action:** only if a feature or spell says "bonus action."
- **Talk:** a short sentence or a shout.
- **Interact:** one small thing, like draw a weapon or open a door.

One time in each round, even on the turn of a different creature, you can
take a **reaction**. A reaction is a fast answer to something, like a Shield
spell or an opportunity attack.

### Actions

| Action | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| Attack | Attack with a weapon. |
| Magic | Cast a spell or use a magic item. |
| Dash | Move one more time. Use it to get far. |
| Disengage | Your move does not let enemies make opportunity attacks this turn. |
| Dodge | Attacks against you have disadvantage until your next turn. |
| Help | Give an ally advantage on their next check or attack. |
| Hide | Make a Stealth check to become hidden. |
| Influence | Try to change the mind of a creature. |
| Search | Look for something with Perception, Insight, Medicine, or Survival. |
| Study | Think about something with Arcana, History, Investigation, Nature, or Religion. |
| Utilize | Use an object, like a healer's kit or a lever. |
| Ready | Choose a trigger. Act when the trigger happens. |

### Opportunity attack

When an enemy that is close to you moves away, you can use your reaction to
make one melee attack against it.

## Attacks and damage

1. Roll a d20 and add your attack number.
2. If the total is equal to or more than the enemy's AC, you hit.
3. Roll the damage dice and add the damage number.
4. **A 20 on the d20 is a critical hit.** Roll the damage dice two times.

## Hit points and 0 HP

- Damage reduces your hit points (HP). Healing increases them, up to your
  maximum.
- At 0 HP, you fall unconscious. You make a **death saving throw** at the
  start of each of your turns:
  - Roll a d20 with no modifier. 10 or more is a success. 9 or less is a
    failure.
  - 3 successes: you are stable. You stop rolling.
  - 3 failures: your character dies.
  - A 20: you wake up with 1 HP.
  - A 1: two failures.
- Any healing wakes you up at once.

## Spells

- A **cantrip** has no cost. Cast it as often as you want.
- A **level 1 spell** uses one **spell slot**. When you have no slots, you
  cannot cast those spells. Slots come back when you rest.
- **Concentration:** you can keep only one concentration spell at a time. If
  you take damage, you make a Constitution saving throw (DC 10, or half the
  damage if that is higher) to keep it.

## Rests

| Rest | Time | What you get back |
|------|------|-------------------|
| Short Rest | 1 hour | Spend Hit Point Dice to heal. Some features come back. |
| Long Rest | 8 hours | All HP, all Hit Point Dice, all spell slots, all features. |

To spend a Hit Point Die, roll it and add your Constitution modifier. You
regain that many HP.
