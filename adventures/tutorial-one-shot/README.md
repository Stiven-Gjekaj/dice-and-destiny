# The Last Watch of the Sleepers

A tutorial one-shot for Dice & Destiny. It teaches new players how to
play. Each scene teaches one rule. Then the players use that rule at once.

- **Players:** 1 to 5. The adventure scales. See [Scaling](#scaling).
- **Level:** 1. At the end, the characters go to level 2.
- **Time:** about 2 hours.
- **Tone:** a spooky crypt, with a funny guide.
- **Characters:** five pregenerated templates. The players give them names.

This adventure is not part of a campaign. You can use it again each time that
you teach a new player.

## Premise

Three hundred years ago, **Malgrath, the Unlit King**, a demon king, tried to
put out the sun. The old kingdom pushed him back into the dark. But the
kingdom knew that he would return one day.

So the kingdom chose five guards, **the Dawnwatch**. A spell put them to sleep
in crystal under **Dawnwatch Keep**. They wait for the day of his return.

Today the crystal cracks. The Unlit King is back. The characters wake up with
stiff legs and a cold head. Their guide is **Wick**, a talking brass lantern.
Wick waited three hundred years for this day, and Wick has a lot to say.

The characters must get out of the keep and reach the surface. On the way,
they learn each rule of the game. At the surface, they find out how much the
world changed while they slept.

## What the players learn

| Scene | Rule | Story lesson |
|-------|------|--------------|
| 0. At the table | The character sheet, the dice | Choose a name |
| 1. The Hall of Sleepers | Ability checks, DC, advantage and disadvantage | The "Before" flashback, Heroic Inspiration |
| 2. Wick | Skills, social checks, Insight | Talk to an NPC |
| 3. The Stair of Teeth | Perception, saving throws, damage and HP | Careful play |
| 4. The Armory | Initiative, turns, actions, movement, attacks, AC | Teamwork |
| 5. A short rest | Short Rest, Hit Point Dice, recovery of features | Bonds between characters |
| 6. The Chapel of Dawn | Spell slots, reactions, 0 HP, death saves | Danger has a cost |
| 7. The Dawn Door | Rewards, level up | The "After" moment |

## Distance

This adventure uses no feet, no squares, and no grid. It uses three words:

| Word | Meaning | Examples |
|------|---------|----------|
| Close | Next to a creature. | A melee attack, a touch, a potion that you give to a friend. |
| Near | In the same room. One move takes you there. | Most movement in a fight. |
| Far | At the other end of a big hall, or out of the room. | A Dash takes you there. |

- A ranged attack or a spell can hit any creature that you can see. You, the
  DM, can say that a target is too far.
- A spell or a breath that hits an area hits a small number of creatures that
  stand close together. The text says how many.
- If a rule in the 2024 books uses feet, use the word that is nearest in
  meaning.

**Solo play:** Give the player the Shield. Wick helps in each fight. Wick has a
Help action for this. In Scene 1, Wick helps the player out of the

## Safety

- Before you start, ask the players: "Is there something that you do not want
  in the game?" Spiders and body horror are common answers. The adventure has
  small demons, fire, and an old crypt. It has no gore.
- Tell the players that they can stop a scene at any time. They tap the
  table or say "pause."
- **No character dies in this tutorial.** The first lesson of the game must not end
  with a lost character. Scene 6 has a rule for this, the Dawnwatch Oath. Do
  not tell the players about it before Scene 6.
