# Pregenerated characters

These are five level 1 characters for the 2024 rules. Each one is a
template. The player writes in a name and keeps the rest.

## How to use these sheets

- Give each player one sheet. Let the player choose a name. Use a suggested
  name if the player cannot choose one.
- Tell the player to read the box "Your best moves" first. The player can
  ignore the other parts until the game needs them.
- Each character was a member of **the Dawnwatch**, five guards of the old
  kingdom. Each sheet has a "Before" question for Scene 1.
- With fewer than five players, use the characters in this order:
  1. The Shield (easiest to play, and best for a solo player)
  2. The Light (heals)
  3. The Spark (magic)
  4. The Shadow (skills)
  5. The Storm (hits hard)
- All characters have a proficiency bonus of +2.

## How to read the numbers

- **Ability scores:** The score is in front. The modifier is in brackets. You
  add the modifier to a d20 roll.
- **Save:** You add this number when the DM asks for a saving throw of that
  ability.
- **Attack:** You add the first number to the d20. If you hit, you roll the
  damage dice and add the second number.

## 1. The Shield

**Name:** ______________ (suggestions: Bren, Kaela, Torvin)

Human Fighter 1. Background: Soldier.

You were the captain of the Dawnwatch. You stand in front, and you take the
hits so that your friends do not.

**Before question:** "The night before the long sleep, you made a promise to
someone. Who was it, and what did you promise?"

### Your best moves

- **Attack** with your longsword: roll d20 + 5. On a hit, deal 1d8 + 5.
- **Second Wind** (bonus action, 2 uses): heal 1d10 + 1 hit points.
- Your armor is heavy. Enemies need a high roll (18) to hit you. Stand
  between them and your friends.

### Numbers

| AC | HP | Speed | Initiative | Passive Perception |
|----|----|-------|------------|--------------------|
| 18 | 14 | 30 ft | +1 | 13 |

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 17 (+3) | 13 (+1) | 15 (+2) | 8 (-1) | 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) |
| Save +5 | Save +1 | Save +4 | Save -1 | Save +1 | Save +0 |

**Skills:** Athletics +5, Insight +3, Intimidation +2, Perception +3,
Survival +3.

**Hit Point Dice:** 1d10.

### Attacks

| Weapon | Attack | Damage | Notes |
|--------|--------|--------|-------|
| Longsword | +5 | 1d8 + 5 slashing | Mastery: Sap |
| Javelin (4) | +5 | 1d6 + 3 piercing | Thrown 30/120 ft. Mastery: Slow |

### Features

- **Fighting Style (Dueling):** When you hold a melee weapon in one hand and no
  other weapon, you add +2 to its damage. This is already in the table.
- **Second Wind:** As a bonus action, you regain 1d10 + 1 hit points. You have
  2 uses. You get 1 use back on a Short Rest, and all uses on a Long Rest.
- **Weapon Mastery:**
  - *Sap (longsword):* When you hit a creature, it has disadvantage on its
    next attack roll before the start of your next turn.
  - *Slow (javelin):* When you hit and deal damage, the target's speed goes
    down by 10 feet until the start of your next turn.
- **Savage Attacker:** One time on each of your turns, when you hit with a
  weapon, you can roll the damage dice two times and use the higher result.
- **Tough:** Your hit point maximum includes +2 for each level. This is
  already in your HP.
- **Resourceful (Human):** You get Heroic Inspiration each time you finish a
  Long Rest.
- **Skillful (Human):** You are proficient in Insight.

### Equipment

Chain mail, shield, longsword, 4 javelins, a set of dice (gaming set), a
backpack, a bedroll, and a rope (50 feet).

### At level 2

- **HP:** +10 (new maximum 24). Hit Point Dice: 2d10.
- **Action Surge:** One time per Short or Long Rest, you take one more action
  on your turn.
- **Tactical Mind:** When you fail an ability check, you can use one Second
  Wind. You roll 1d10 and add it to the check. If you still fail, you keep the
  use.

## 2. The Light

**Name:** ______________ (suggestions: Hilda, Dorn, Brakka)

Dwarf Cleric 1. Background: Acolyte.

You were the healer of the Dawnwatch. Your god is the Morning Flame, the god
of the dawn. You keep your friends alive.

**Before question:** "When the crystal closed over you, your god sent you a
sign. What did you see?"

### Your best moves

- **Healing Word** (bonus action, uses a spell slot): an ally that you can see
  within 60 feet heals 2d4 + 3. This also wakes up an ally at 0 HP.
- **Sacred Flame** (action, no slot): one enemy within 60 feet makes a
  Dexterity saving throw (DC 13). On a fail, it takes 1d8 radiant damage.
- **Spare the Dying** (action, no slot): an ally at 0 HP within 15 feet
  becomes stable and stops making death saving throws.

### Numbers

| AC | HP | Speed | Initiative | Passive Perception |
|----|----|-------|------------|--------------------|
| 18 | 11 | 30 ft | +0 | 13 |

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 13 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 8 (-1) | 17 (+3) | 13 (+1) |
| Save +1 | Save +0 | Save +2 | Save -1 | Save +5 | Save +3 |

**Skills:** Insight +5, Medicine +5, Persuasion +3, Religion +1.

**Hit Point Dice:** 1d8.

**Darkvision:** 120 feet. You see in the dark as if it is dim light.

### Attacks

| Weapon | Attack | Damage | Notes |
|--------|--------|--------|-------|
| Mace | +3 | 1d6 + 1 bludgeoning | |

### Spellcasting

- **Spell save DC:** 13. **Spell attack:** +5.
- **Spell slots (level 1):** 2. You get all slots back on a Long Rest.
- **Cantrips (no slot, use them as often as you want):** Sacred Flame,
  Guidance, Spare the Dying, Light, Thaumaturgy.
- **Prepared level 1 spells (each cast uses one slot):**
  - *Healing Word:* bonus action, range 60 feet. One creature regains
    2d4 + 3 HP.
  - *Cure Wounds:* action, touch. One creature regains 2d8 + 3 HP.
  - *Guiding Bolt:* action, range 120 feet. Make a spell attack. On a hit,
    deal 4d6 radiant damage. The next attack roll against the target has
    advantage.
  - *Shield of Faith:* bonus action, range 60 feet, concentration. One
    creature gets +2 AC for up to 10 minutes.
- **Free spell (Magic Initiate):** You can cast *Bless* one time without a
  slot. You get this back on a Long Rest. *Bless:* action, concentration. Up
  to 3 creatures add 1d4 to their attack rolls and saving throws for up to 1
  minute.

### Features

- **Divine Order (Protector):** You can wear heavy armor and use martial
  weapons.
- **Dwarven Resilience:** You have resistance to poison damage. You have
  advantage on saving throws against the poisoned condition.
- **Dwarven Toughness:** Your hit point maximum includes +1 for each level.
  This is already in your HP.
- **Stonecunning:** As a bonus action, you get tremorsense out to 60 feet for
  10 minutes, when you stand on stone. You have 2 uses. You get them back on a
  Long Rest.

### Equipment

Chain mail, shield, mace, holy symbol (a sun disk), a healer's kit, a backpack,
calligrapher's supplies, and a book of prayers.

### At level 2

- **HP:** +8 (new maximum 19). Hit Point Dice: 2d8.
- **Spell slots:** 3. You prepare 1 more level 1 spell (for example, *Bless*
  or *Command*).
- **Channel Divinity (2 uses):** You get 1 use back on a Short Rest.
  - *Divine Spark:* As a Magic action, one creature within 30 feet regains
    1d8 + 3 HP, or makes a Constitution saving throw (DC 13) and takes 1d8
    radiant damage (half on a success).
  - *Turn Undead:* Undead near you can flee from you.

## 3. The Spark

**Name:** ______________ (suggestions: Ilyra, Faen, Soren)

Elf Wizard 1. Background: Sage.

You were the mage of the Dawnwatch, and the youngest member ever. You know
much about magic and old stories. You have low HP, so stay behind your
friends.

**Before question:** "On your first day in the Dawnwatch, you showed off a
spell. What happened?"

### Your best moves

- **Fire Bolt** (action, no slot): make a spell attack (d20 + 5) against one
  enemy within 120 feet. On a hit, deal 1d10 fire damage.
- **Magic Missile** (action, uses a slot): three darts hit automatically. Each
  dart deals 1d4 + 1 force damage. You can send all darts at one enemy, or
  split them.
- **Shield** (reaction, uses a slot): when an attack hits you, you get +5 AC
  until the start of your next turn. This can make the attack miss.

### Numbers

| AC | HP | Speed | Initiative | Passive Perception |
|----|----|-------|------------|--------------------|
| 11 (14 with Mage Armor) | 8 | 30 ft | +1 | 13 |

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 8 (-1) | 13 (+1) | 15 (+2) | 17 (+3) | 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) |
| Save -1 | Save +1 | Save +2 | Save +5 | Save +3 | Save +0 |

**Skills:** Arcana +5, History +5, Insight +3, Investigation +5,
Perception +3.

**Hit Point Dice:** 1d6.

**Darkvision:** 60 feet.

### Attacks

| Weapon | Attack | Damage | Notes |
|--------|--------|--------|-------|
| Dagger | +3 | 1d4 + 1 piercing | Thrown 20/60 ft |
| Fire Bolt (cantrip) | +5 | 1d10 fire | Range 120 ft |
| Ray of Frost (cantrip) | +5 | 1d8 cold | Range 60 ft. The target's speed goes down by 10 feet |

### Spellcasting

- **Spell save DC:** 13. **Spell attack:** +5.
- **Spell slots (level 1):** 2. You get all slots back on a Long Rest.
- **Cantrips (no slot):** Fire Bolt, Ray of Frost, Light, Mage Hand, Minor
  Illusion, Prestidigitation.
- **Prepared level 1 spells (each cast uses one slot):**
  - *Magic Missile:* action, range 120 feet. Three darts, each 1d4 + 1 force
    damage. They always hit.
  - *Shield:* reaction when an attack hits you. +5 AC until the start of your
    next turn.
  - *Burning Hands:* action, 15-foot cone. Each creature in the cone makes a
    Dexterity saving throw (DC 13). It takes 3d6 fire damage on a fail, or
    half on a success.
  - *Sleep:* action, range 60 feet, concentration. Each creature of your
    choice in a 5-foot-radius sphere makes a Wisdom saving throw (DC 13). On
    a fail, it is incapacitated until the end of its next turn. Then it makes
    the save again. On a second fail, it falls unconscious for up to 1
    minute, until it takes damage or someone shakes it awake.
- **Free spell (Magic Initiate):** You can cast *Mage Armor* one time without
  a slot. You get this back on a Long Rest. *Mage Armor:* your AC becomes 14
  for 8 hours. Cast it at the start of the adventure.
- **Spellbook:** Magic Missile, Shield, Burning Hands, Sleep, Detect Magic,
  Find Familiar.

### Features

- **Arcane Recovery:** When you finish a Short Rest, you get back one level 1
  spell slot. You can do this one time per day.
- **Ritual Adept:** You can cast a spell with the Ritual tag from your
  spellbook. It takes 10 minutes more, and it does not use a slot.
- **Fey Ancestry (Elf):** You have advantage on saving throws against the
  charmed condition.
- **Keen Senses (Elf):** You are proficient in Perception.
- **Trance (Elf):** You do not sleep. You finish a Long Rest in 4 hours.

### Equipment

Dagger, quarterstaff, spellbook, arcane focus (a crystal wand), a backpack,
a bottle of ink, a quill, and calligrapher's supplies.

### At level 2

- **HP:** +6 (new maximum 14). Hit Point Dice: 2d6.
- **Spell slots:** 3. You prepare 1 more level 1 spell (for example, *Detect
  Magic* or *Find Familiar*).
- **Scholar:** Choose Arcana, History, or Investigation. You add double your
  proficiency bonus to that skill.

## 4. The Shadow

**Name:** ______________ (suggestions: Nim, Tansy, Corrin)

Halfling Rogue 1. Background: Criminal.

You were the scout of the Dawnwatch. Before that, you were a thief. The
captain gave you a second chance. You find traps, open locks, and hit hard
when an enemy does not see you.

**Before question:** "You stole one last thing on the night before the long
sleep. What was it?" (DM: it is still in your pocket.)

### Your best moves

- **Attack** with your shortsword (d20 + 5, damage 1d6 + 3) or your shortbow
  (d20 + 5, damage 1d6 + 3, range 80 feet).
- **Sneak Attack:** one time on each of your turns, add 1d6 damage if you have
  advantage, or if an ally stands next to the target.
- **Skills:** you are the best at Stealth (+7) and Perception (+5). Ask the DM
  if you can search for traps.

### Numbers

| AC | HP | Speed | Initiative | Passive Perception |
|----|----|-------|------------|--------------------|
| 14 | 10 | 30 ft | +5 | 15 |

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 8 (-1) | 17 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 12 (+1) | 13 (+1) | 10 (+0) |
| Save -1 | Save +5 | Save +2 | Save +3 | Save +1 | Save +0 |

**Skills:** Acrobatics +5, Investigation +3, Perception +5 (expertise),
Persuasion +2, Sleight of Hand +5, Stealth +7 (expertise).

**Hit Point Dice:** 1d8.

### Attacks

| Weapon | Attack | Damage | Notes |
|--------|--------|--------|-------|
| Shortsword | +5 | 1d6 + 3 piercing | Mastery: Vex |
| Shortbow | +5 | 1d6 + 3 piercing | Range 80/320 ft. Mastery: Vex |
| Dagger (2) | +5 | 1d4 + 3 piercing | Thrown 20/60 ft |

### Features

- **Sneak Attack:** One time on each of your turns, you deal 1d6 more damage
  when you hit with a finesse or ranged weapon, if one of these is true:
  - You have advantage on the attack roll.
  - An ally is within 5 feet of the target, and you do not have
    disadvantage.
- **Expertise:** You add double your proficiency bonus to Stealth and
  Perception. This is already in your skills.
- **Weapon Mastery (Vex):** When you hit and deal damage, you have advantage
  on your next attack roll against that creature before the end of your next
  turn. This gives you Sneak Attack on the next attack, too.
- **Thieves' Cant:** You know the secret language of thieves.
- **Alert:** You add your proficiency bonus to initiative. When you roll
  initiative, you can swap your result with a willing ally.
- **Luck (Halfling):** When you roll a 1 on a d20 for an attack roll, an
  ability check, or a saving throw, you roll the die again. You must use the
  new roll.
- **Brave (Halfling):** You have advantage on saving throws against the
  frightened condition.
- **Halfling Nimbleness:** You can move through the space of a creature that
  is larger than you.
- **Naturally Stealthy:** You can try to hide behind a creature that is
  larger than you.

### Equipment

Leather armor, shortsword, shortbow and 20 arrows, 2 daggers, thieves' tools,
a dark hooded cloak, a backpack, and a crowbar.

### At level 2

- **HP:** +7 (new maximum 17). Hit Point Dice: 2d8.
- **Cunning Action:** On each of your turns, you can Dash, Disengage, or Hide
  as a bonus action.
